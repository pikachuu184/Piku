# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include showing empathy, being
respectful of differing viewpoints, and accepting constructive criticism. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact details in the
repository's GitHub profile. Maintainers will review reports and may remove comments, close issues,
or restrict participation when necessary.
